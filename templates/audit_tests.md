# Tests audit

Read project configuration, the active prompt and available checks from the inventory. Trace acceptance criteria to meaningful tests or measured evidence. Identify untested failure paths, misleading assertions, mocks replacing the subject under test, races, global profile writes, platform assumptions and coverage gaps.

Run appropriate real checks in isolated fixtures. Keep owner accounts and profiles untouched. Report exactly what passed, failed, was skipped and remains unverified. Coverage of lines and branches are distinct claims; do not substitute one for the other. CI availability does not replace local evidence.

Write the research report, machine ledger and repair prompt in their configured roles and language. Follow the production audit ledger contract. Preserve substantive evidence for each fixed or rejected finding.
