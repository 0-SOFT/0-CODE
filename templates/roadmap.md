# Roadmap

Use the configured roadmap role and the project's existing structure. State the target, dependencies and order of remaining versions. Keep each version small enough to verify. For each version include a concrete goal, scope, acceptance criteria and status.

Acceptance criteria of released versions must carry evidence or an explicit, substantive explanation of non-completion. Never tick a box merely to satisfy a gate.

Review the remaining plan against actual findings at the cadence in the state. Remove, split or reorder work when evidence warrants it. Write `roadmap_reviewed_at_iteration` and a substantive `roadmap_review_note` together; an unchanged plan still needs a statement of what was checked and why it holds.
